<Lead>Your plan is yours to change: every day, every exercise.</Lead>

Each day wears a plate colour and a symbol. Red days push, blue days pull, yellow days train legs, and grey covers the rest.

<DayKinds />

The lifts a split starts with are not random. Each template follows a well-known program, named under it in the picker: the Reddit PPL, Jeff Nippard's PPL + Upper / Lower, Brandon Campbell's PHUL, the classic Upper / Lower, full-body and bodybuilding splits, and Arnold's. They keep what the research on muscle growth agrees on: every muscle twice a week on four days or more, the big compound lifts first, and 10 to 20 hard sets a week on the big muscles. Prefer your own lifts? Tick **Start with empty days** when you pick a split and fill each day in here.

<Facts>
  <Fact term="Edit an exercise">Tap it to change its name, sets, reps or type, or swap in one of its alternates. A lift the library knows comes with the sets and range it is usually done for, on a swap and when you pick it from the library; change them if your plan says otherwise.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Add and arrange">**Add exercise** adds one, up to ten a day. Drag a lift by its grip to reorder the day; **Restore default order** in the day's menu puts it back. **Add a day** adds a day, and each day's menu renames, moves or removes it.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Your own file">**Bring your own plan** loads a plan from Excel or CSV. **Download template** gives you a file to fill in, starting from your current plan or any split. A day is its rows in the sheet, so a day with no lifts does not travel through the file.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Empty days">A day with no lifts shows **Add lifts on Plan** on Today instead of Start. Removing the last lift of a day leaves it that way until you add one.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Start over">**Reset to the original plan** undoes your edits to a built-in split, and brings its lifts back to days you started empty.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Change split">In Settings. Each split keeps its own plan, so going back brings your edits back. **Start with empty days** gives the picked split, or the one you are on, its days with no lifts in place of that plan.</Fact>
</Facts>
</WithShot>
