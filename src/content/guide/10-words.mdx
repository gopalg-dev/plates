export const part = 'Reference'

<Lead>New to lifting? These are the words the app uses.</Lead>

<Facts>
  <Fact term="Rep and set">A rep is one lift of the weight. A set is reps in a row before a rest. 3 × 8 means 3 sets of 8 reps.</Fact>
  <Fact term="60 kg × 8">8 reps at 60 kg.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Rep range">The reps to aim for in each set, like 8–12. Each exercise has one, set as Low and High on Plan.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Target">What a set asks for today, worked out from your last sessions.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Split">How training is divided into days. Push / Pull / Legs and Upper / Lower are splits.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Push, pull, legs">Push days train chest, shoulders and triceps. Pull days train back, rear shoulders and biceps. Legs days train thighs, hips and calves.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Upper, lower, full">The upper half, the legs and hips, or everything in one day.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Weighted lift">A lift with a bar, dumbbells or a machine. A bodyweight lift is you lifting yourself, as in a pull-up.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Alternate">A swap an exercise keeps ready, for when the machine is taken.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Plate">A weight disc that slides onto a bar.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Hard set">A set you ticked done. Stats counts them per muscle each week.</Fact>
  <Fact term="One-rep max">The most you could lift once. The app estimates it from weight and reps to compare sets.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Deload">A lighter week to recover, with fewer sets at the same weights.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Training age">How long you have trained, set in Settings.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Gain, hold, cut">Your bodyweight goal: put weight on, keep it, or lose it.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Tape">Body measurements with a tape measure, at the arm, chest and waist.</Fact>
</Facts>
