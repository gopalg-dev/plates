**How to measure.** The tape only tells you something when every entry is taken the same way. Same side, same time of day, before training, tape snug but not digging in.

<Facts>
  <Fact term="Arm">Bend the arm and flex. Wrap the tape around the peak of the upper arm, straight across, not on a slant. Always the same arm.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Chest">Arms down, tape under the armpits around the fullest part. Breathe out normally, then read it.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Waist">At the navel. Belly relaxed, not pulled in, after breathing out.</Fact>
</Facts>
