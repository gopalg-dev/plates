Chips on the Lifts tab filter your lifts by trend:

<TrendShapes />

**Idle** means no session for that lift in 14 days, and **New** means less than three weeks of history. A lift has one trend at a time.

**Report**, in the menu, is the look back: a finished week or month next to the one before. Today links to it for two days after a week or a month ends.

<Facts>
  <Fact term="The numbers">Sessions against your plan, hard sets, new bests and bodyweight, each against the period before.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Needs attention">Muscles clearly under the hard sets your plan asks for (less than nine in ten), lifts stuck or slipping, sessions rated **Too much**, and two hard weeks with no lift moving. Shown only when there is something.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Went well">New bests in the period, and lifts whose best set beat the period before.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Share">**Share**, in the menu beside the title, makes an image of the week: your days, the sessions, and one highlight you pick, a new best, how many, the streak, every session done, or nothing, in any of the looks. Bodyweight stays off it unless you tick it.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Finished periods only">The week or month still running is never reported on. A week needs one session, a month three. The arrows reach back a year, and the period name opens a calendar to jump further.</Fact>
</Facts>
