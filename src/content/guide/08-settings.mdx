<Lead>You set most of these during setup. A few are worth a second look.</Lead>

<Facts>
  <Fact term="Schedule">**Rolling** runs the days in order whenever you train. **By weekday** ties each day to a weekday you pick on Plan. Today then shows today's day or a rest day.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Training age">**Under a year** or **A year or more**. It does not count up on its own, so change it once you pass a year.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Bodyweight goal">Gain, hold or cut. The Body tab reads your pace against it.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Look">The app's colours, in the account menu: tap your name at the bottom of the side menu. On a phone, open the side menu from the top left.</Fact>
  <Fact term="Feedback">**Send feedback** sits in the same menu.</Fact>
</Facts>
